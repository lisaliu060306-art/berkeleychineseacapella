# ✨ 伯村清唱团 (Berkeley Chinese A Cappella) ✨

Welcome to the official website of UC Berkeley's FIRST and ONLY Chinese a cappella group! 🎤🎶

## 🌟 Who We Are
We're a family of passionate singers bringing the beauty of Chinese music to Berkeley through pure, golden harmonies. From timeless classics to modern pop, we weave together voices to create something magical.

## 🎵 What You'll Find Here
- **Meet the crew** - Our amazing members
- **Join us** - Audition info and FAQs
- **Catch us live** - Upcoming performance dates
- **Book us** - Performance request form for your next event
- **Relive moments** - Photo gallery from our shows
